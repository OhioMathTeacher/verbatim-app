ACTIVITY — Why are soup cans that shape?
Maxima, minima and shape: optimization (AP topics 5.10-5.11).
Fixed volume 400 cm^3. Surface area A = 2 pi r^2 + 2V/r; least at
r = (V/(2 pi))^(1/3), about 3.99 cm, with height = 2r, about 7.99 cm: as tall
as it is wide. Real food cans are usually taller than they are wide. If the
ends cost k times as much per cm^2 as the side, the best height is 2kr.
