Verbatim and students under 18

Verbatim is used in school classes, including with students under 18. It has no accounts and no sign-in, and it asks for no name.

A student's conversation is saved only in their own browser until they download it and hand it in to their teacher.

During an activity, messages pass through a relay that keeps no copy, to Anthropic's Claude, which does not use them for training. The page tells students they are talking with an AI. The AI's instructions in every activity tell it to keep replies suitable for a classroom, and to send a student who may be at risk to their teacher or another trusted adult.

Verbatim is not directed to children under 13. Each school keeps control of its students' records.
